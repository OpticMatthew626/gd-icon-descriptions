GD Icons and Their Descriptions

Cube 1:

This icon is the 1st icon in the game. It is the one

everybody starts with. It consists of a square with

a smaller sqaure in the middle.

Cube 2:

This cube comes with the game as well as the 1st cube.

It looks like Robtop's icon, except the mouth

is lower than his.

Cube 3:

This icon also comes with the game by default. It is

most famous for being the Youtuber Partition's icon.

The icon consists of two left and right pieces that

connect with eachother.

Cube 4:

This icon is Robtop's icon and it is the last icon that comes with the

game. It is like Cube 2, except it looks less derpy because the mouth

larger and located higher on the face.
