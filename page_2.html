GD Icons and Their Descriptions

Cube 5:

This icon is the 1st icon in the game. It is the one

everybody starts with. It consists of a square with

a smaller sqaure in the middle.

Cube 6:

This cube comes with the game as well as the 1st cube.

It looks like Robtop's icon, except the mouth

is lower than his.
